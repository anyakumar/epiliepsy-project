Dataset Information

Primary Dataset: UCI Epileptic Seizure Recognition

The dataset consists of EEG recordings represented as 178 numerical features per sample. Each sample captures a 1-second recording of brain activity.

11,500 Total Samples
178 Features
5 Classes
23 Subjects

Class Labels & Recording Conditions

Class 1

Seizure Activity

Recording of seizure activity in epileptic subjects.

Class 2

Tumor Area

Recorded from tumor area (No seizure).

Class 3

Healthy Area

Healthy brain area in tumor subjects.

Class 4

Eyes Closed

Healthy subject with eyes closed.

Class 5

Eyes Open

Healthy subject with eyes open.

Source & Credits

Sourced from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. Original study by Andrzejak et al. (2001).

policy

Ethical Note & Privacy

This dataset contains heavily processed and anonymized numerical data. No patient-identifiable information (PII) is included or accessible. The data is used strictly for academic research and educational model benchmarking.
